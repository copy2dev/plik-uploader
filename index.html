Plik Uploader

Plik Uploader is a private command-line tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Plik (@plikstory) to upload and schedule the channel's own videos through the YouTube API Services.

It uploads finished videos and Shorts as private with a scheduled publish time, sets the owner's thumbnails and captions, and adds the videos to the owner's playlists. It is not offered to other users and has no public sign-up.
